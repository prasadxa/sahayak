# Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY): Crop Insurance for Farmers

PMFBY (also called Fasal Bima Yojana or crop insurance) is the Government of India's crop insurance scheme. It protects farmers against crop loss from natural calamities, pests and diseases. This note explains the scheme in simple words.

## What is PMFBY?

PMFBY is a crop insurance scheme launched in 2016. The farmer pays a small part of the premium and the Central and State governments pay the rest. If the crop is damaged by covered risks, the insurance company pays a claim into the farmer's bank account.

## Who can take PMFBY crop insurance?

All farmers growing the **notified crops** in **notified areas** can enrol, including **sharecroppers and tenant farmers**. Each state notifies which crops and areas are covered for each season, so check with your bank, agriculture office or the PMFBY portal.

## Is PMFBY compulsory for farmers with a crop loan?

No. Since **kharif 2020**, the scheme is **voluntary for all farmers**, including those who have a crop loan or Kisan Credit Card. Earlier it was compulsory for loanee farmers. A loanee farmer who does not want insurance must inform the bank in writing before the cut-off date for that season; otherwise the bank may enrol them.

## How much premium does the farmer pay?

The farmer pays a fixed small share of the **sum insured**:

| Season / crop type | Farmer's premium |
| --- | --- |
| Kharif crops (food grains and oilseeds) | **2%** of sum insured |
| Rabi crops (food grains and oilseeds) | **1.5%** of sum insured |
| Annual commercial and horticultural crops | **5%** of sum insured |

The rest of the actual premium is paid by the Central and State governments.

### Example: how is the premium calculated?

If the sum insured for your crop is ₹1,00,000:

- kharif crop: 2% of ₹1,00,000 = **₹2,000**
- rabi crop: 1.5% of ₹1,00,000 = **₹1,500**
- annual commercial or horticultural crop: 5% of ₹1,00,000 = **₹5,000**

Sahayak can do this calculation for you. Just tell it the season and the sum insured.

## How is the sum insured decided?

The sum insured per hectare is generally linked to the **scale of finance** for that crop, which is fixed at the district level. It is multiplied by the area you insure. The exact sum insured for your crop and district is shown on the PMFBY portal and at your bank.

## What risks are covered?

PMFBY covers:

- **Standing crop (yield loss):** loss due to non-preventable risks such as drought, dry spells, flood, inundation, pests and diseases, landslides, natural fire, lightning, storm, hailstorm and cyclone.
- **Prevented sowing:** when bad weather stops farmers in an area from sowing.
- **Localised calamities:** damage to individual farms from events like hailstorm, landslide, inundation, cloudburst and natural fire due to lightning.
- **Post-harvest losses:** damage to a harvested crop kept in the field to dry ("cut and spread"), for up to two weeks after harvest, due to hailstorm, cyclone, cyclonic rain or unseasonal rain.

Losses from war, nuclear risks, malicious damage and other preventable risks are not covered.

## How do I enrol in PMFBY?

You can enrol through:

1. your **bank branch** (the bank where you have your crop loan or savings account);
2. a **Common Service Centre (CSC)** near you, many of which are now run by PACS;
3. the **PMFBY portal** (pmfby.gov.in) or the **Crop Insurance app**; or
4. an **insurance intermediary** or the agent of the insurance company for your area.

Enrolment must be done **before the cut-off date** notified for each season and state. These dates change every season, so check them on the PMFBY portal or at your bank.

## What documents do I need to enrol?

Usually you need:

- Aadhaar card;
- bank passbook or account details (the claim is paid into this account);
- land records, or the tenancy or sharecropping agreement or certificate as required by your state;
- a sowing declaration or certificate for the crop you have sown, where asked.

Keep the **receipt or application number** safely. You will need it to check your policy and claim.

## What should I do if my crop is damaged?

For **localised calamity** or **post-harvest loss**, report it within **72 hours** of the event. You can report through:

- the **Crop Insurance app**;
- the **insurance company** for your area (its toll-free number is on your policy receipt);
- your **bank** or the **agriculture department** office; or
- the **Krishi Rakshak helpline 14447**.

Give your name, village, crop, survey or khasra number, the date of damage and the policy or application number. Keep photos of the damage if you can. The insurance company will assess the loss.

For widespread yield loss in a whole area, the claim is usually worked out from crop cutting experiments and yield data for that area, so an individual report may not be needed. Still, informing the insurer or agriculture office is a good idea.

## How and when is the claim paid?

Claims are paid directly into the farmer's **bank account** that is linked to the policy. You can check the status of your application and claim on the PMFBY portal or the Crop Insurance app using your application number. If your claim is delayed or rejected, contact the insurance company, then your bank or district agriculture office, or call 14447.

## Does every state run PMFBY?

Not every state. Some states run their own crop insurance or farmer support schemes instead of PMFBY, or join only for some seasons. Check with your district agriculture office.

## Key points to remember

- Farmer premium: kharif 2%, rabi 1.5%, annual commercial or horticultural 5% of the sum insured.
- Voluntary for all farmers since kharif 2020.
- Enrol through a bank, a CSC, the PMFBY portal or app, or an insurance intermediary, before the cut-off date.
- Report localised or post-harvest loss within 72 hours through the Crop Insurance app, the insurer or helpline 14447.
